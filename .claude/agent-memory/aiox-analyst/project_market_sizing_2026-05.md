Market sizing for Gestar em Movimento completed 2026-05-28 (analyst: Atlas). Extends [[project_business_audit_2026-05]].

**Anchored data (sourced, not assumed):**
- Brazil live births: ~2.5M/year (Registro Civil/IBGE 2023), declining trend. Pregnancies ~2.5-3M/yr.
- Fertility rate: 1.6 (below 2.1 replacement) — TAM is SHRINKING, not growing. Material headwind.
- Smartphone users: ~150M+ in Brazil.
- Global femtech: ~USD 51-60B (2023-24), CAGR 15-18% to 2030. LatAm is a small slice; Brazil largest in LatAm.
- Wellness app freemium→paid conversion: Brazil 2-3% (low end of global 1-5%), price-sensitive.
- Pricing benchmark: successful BR wellness apps R$9,90-R$29,90/mo. Pix + parcelamento materially lift conversion. Medical endorsement lifts willingness-to-pay.

**Sizing output (bottom-up, annual):**
- TAM ~R$ 360M/yr (≈3M pregnant women × ~R$120 annualized value if all paid a wellness sub).
- SAM ~R$ 110-130M/yr (smartphone-using, engaged-wellness, fitness-receptive segment ≈ 30-40% of TAM).
- SOM (realistic 3-yr) ~R$ 300K-900K/yr (niche fisio/médico-endorsed channel, low-thousands paying users at 2-3% conversion).

**Key insight:** This is a NICHE play, not a venture-scale TAM. Declining birth rate + price sensitivity + niche wedge (exercise) cap upside. Best path is B2B2C (clínicas/fisioterapeutas/planos) NOT pure D2C subscription scale.

**How to apply:** Frame to user as "lifestyle/niche profitable business or B2B2C channel play" — NOT a unicorn TAM. Anchor any monetization model at 2-3% conversion, R$/Pix pricing, with medical endorsement as the conversion lever.
