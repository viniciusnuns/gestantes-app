Business audit of "Gestar em Movimento" completed 2026-05-27 (analyst: Atlas).

**Headline verdict:** Opportunity score 6/10. Problem/Solution fit = YELLOW (Uncertain). The core risk is NOT technical — it is commercial/regulatory: (1) no validated willingness-to-pay, (2) gamification/ranking is high-risk for the pregnant audience (competitive ranking can be a liability with a medically anxious, non-homogeneous cohort — a Strava-style leaderboard between women at different gestational weeks/risk levels is a clinical and ethical hazard), (3) regulated health-claims territory (ANVISA SaMD + LGPD sensitive data).

**Why:** The product diferencial (exercícios personalizados por semana + ranking) competes in a crowded info/community space (BabyCenter, Gravidez+ / WTE) where the strongest retention driver is informational/community, not fitness. Exercise is a *narrow wedge*, not yet validated as the primary willingness-to-pay driver.

**How to apply:** When advising on roadmap, push validation (médico/fisio endorsement + 100-user retention test) BEFORE monetization or scaling features. Recommend reframing "ranking" as personal-progress/streaks, not inter-user competition. Treat ANVISA classification + LGPD sensitive-data consent as gating items, not backlog items. See [[project_gestar_em_movimento]] and [[project_infra_stack]].

**Open data gaps the team must close:** willingness-to-pay survey, ginecologista/fisioterapeuta interviews (n>=5), confirmation of whether exercise vs information vs community is the #1 user need.
